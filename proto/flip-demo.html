1 · No re-orientation beat

After a flip, can you read your arc and commit to the next grab immediately — or is there a beat of “wait, where am I”?

2 · Comfortable at rate

Hold F down and flip as fast as it will go for a minute. Try the ease at 0 ms and at 500 ms. Where does it stop being okay?

3 · Misses feel like yours

When you sail past an arrow into the boundary, do you know why you missed — or did it feel arbitrary?

The square’s problem, to see for yourself: a 720 px square rotated 45° needs 1018 px of height and the viewport has 720. Switch to Square, flip, and watch the corners leave the screen — anything in them, including you, is invisible for the length of the ease. The circle has no such angle.

What this is not: a throwaway prototype in JavaScript, built to settle the room-shape question before the real work starts. The physics is a faithful port — semi-implicit Euler at 240 Hz, F = k·r, your tuned values — but the real slice 2 is Python, and none of this code ships.
